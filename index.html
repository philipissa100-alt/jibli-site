Parcels carried by people
already flying your route.

Somebody is flying where your parcel needs to go, with space in their bag. JIBLI introduces you, keeps the record of what was agreed, and stays out of the way. No courier, no custody, no middleman holding your money.

Sending something

Search your route or post your parcel and let travelers come to you. Hand it over at their pickup point, track it, and pay cash at the door when it arrives.

Carrying something

Post your flight, choose what to take, and the spare space in your bag is worth something. You see the weight, the size and what it is before you decide, and you can refuse anything at the meeting point without giving a reason.

The six digits

Every parcel has a code that only the person receiving it holds. The delivery closes when they give it to the traveler. That is the whole mechanism, and it is why nobody has to trust anybody in advance.
