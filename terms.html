Terms

Last updated 29 October 2026

JIBLI connects people who are flying with people who want something carried. We are the introduction and the record of it. We are not a courier, we do not take custody of anything, and we do not handle the money.

What we do and do not do

We match a parcel to a traveler, give you a place to talk, and hold the record of what was agreed. The parcel goes from your hands to theirs and from theirs to the recipient. Nobody at JIBLI ever touches it.

The money is between you

Payment is cash, paid to the traveler on delivery, at the door. JIBLI does not hold it, does not process it, and cannot refund it. The whole reward goes to the traveler. We take nothing from it today, and if that ever changes we will tell you before it does rather than after.

The six digits

A delivery is closed when the recipient gives the traveler their code and the traveler enters it. Do not share your code before you have what you are waiting for. Entering a code you were given without having delivered is fraud, and it is the one thing that ends an account without warning.

What you may not send

Anything illegal at either end of the trip, anything a traveler could be arrested for carrying, and anything you have not described honestly. A traveler may open and inspect a parcel before accepting it, and should. If you would not say what is inside out loud at a check-in desk, do not send it.

You are carrying it, not us

A traveler who agrees to carry something is responsible for it as their own luggage, and is subject to the customs and airline rules of both countries. Check what you are taking. Refuse anything that does not match what was described.

How people behave here

JIBLI only works because strangers meet in person. There is no tolerance for abusive, threatening, harassing, hateful or discriminatory behaviour, in a message or at a handover, and none for content of that kind anywhere in the app. Report it and we will act: we can read the thread, we can suspend an account, and we do not require you to have proof before you tell us. Blocking somebody is always available and never has to be explained.

If something goes wrong

Tell us. We can see the record of what was agreed, when, and by whom, and we will act on it. What we cannot do is compensate you, because at no point did we hold your parcel or your money.

Your account

One account per person, aged 18 or over, with a real name that other people will see. You can delete it whenever you like. We can suspend one for fraud, for a false code, for abusive behaviour, or for sending something that should not have been sent.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that affects what you agreed to, we will ask you again rather than quietly updating this page.
