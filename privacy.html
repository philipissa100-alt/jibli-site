Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

The short version: we keep what the app needs to match a parcel to a traveler and to show two strangers enough about each other to say yes. We do not sell any of it.

What you give us

Your name, your email, your phone number, and whatever else you choose to add to your profile: a photo, your birthdate, gender, what you do, a line about yourself, and a home city. The name is required because other people see it. The phone number is required because whoever meets you at a handover is given it, and it is never shown on your profile. Everything else is optional and you can remove it. We store the birthdate rather than an age, because an age typed once is wrong a year later.

What the app records as you use it

The flights you post, the parcels you request, who accepted what and when, your messages, and your delivery codes. This is the record of what was agreed, and it is the only thing we can look at when something goes wrong.

Who sees what

Your first name, last initial, photo, rating and delivery count are visible to anyone who can see your flight or your parcel. Your email is never shown to another user. Your full last name is never shown to anyone you have not met. A request posted to friends is visible only to your friends, and one posted to a group only to that group. That is enforced by the database, not by what the app chooses to display.

Your messages

Stored so that both of you can read the thread. Not scanned, not used for advertising. We can read them if we are investigating a report, and we would rather tell you that here than not.

ID verification

If you verify your identity, we keep the result and the fact of it. Other people see a tick, never the document.

Where it lives

On Supabase, which hosts the database and handles sign-in. Push notifications go through Expo. Those are the only two places your data goes.

Deleting it

Ask and we remove your account and your profile, or delete it yourself from inside the app. Completed deliveries keep a record of having happened, because the other person was part of it too and it is their history as much as yours.

Children

JIBLI is for people aged 18 and over. We ask for a birthdate when an account is created and do not knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If you believe a child has an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Getting in touch

Any question about your data, including a request to see it or delete it, goes to philipissa100@gmail.com.
