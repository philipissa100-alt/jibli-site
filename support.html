Support

JIBLI is a small team. Write to philipissa100@gmail.com and you will normally hear back within two working days.

Something went wrong with a delivery

Open the delivery in Dashboard and use Report. That attaches the record of what was agreed to your message, which is the thing we can actually act on. If you cannot reach the app, email us with the two names and roughly when it happened.

Somebody is behaving badly

Report them from their profile or from the chat thread, and block them if you want them gone immediately. Blocking hides each of you from the other and never has to be explained. You do not need proof before telling us.

I cannot get into my account

Email us from the address you signed up with and we will help you back in.

I want my data, or I want it deleted

You can delete your account from Profile. For anything else, including a copy of what we hold, write to philipissa100@gmail.com.

Anything else

Write to philipissa100@gmail.com. One person reads it, so it will not be a form letter.
